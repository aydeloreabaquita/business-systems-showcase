## Questions for the first conversation

1. Where do new enquiries arrive today, and who owns the first response?
2. Which handoff most often requires chasing or re-entering information?
3. What data do you actually need to capture at that stage?
4. Who should be allowed to view or change each record?
5. Which existing calendar, accounting, quoting, or operational tool must remain the source of truth?
6. What measurable pilot outcome would make this worthwhile?

## Company-specific discovery

- **Skinov8ive:** Discuss administrative enquiries and appointment coordination without requesting clinical information. Confirm booking, consent, and privacy requirements before proposing any integration.
- **Arbor:** Ask about specification fields, units, grades, quantities, currencies, quote approvals, and fulfilment handoffs. This demo does not calculate timber prices or maintain stock.
- **Tri-Con:** Ask about scope capture, attachments, estimate approval, site reviews, and scheduling dependencies. This demo does not produce engineering or construction estimates.
- **SMOX:** Confirm business identity, services, region, service frequency, assignment process, and the appropriate operations contact. This demo does not assess applicants or make employment decisions.

## Pilot scope suggestion

Begin with one enquiry form, one owner assignment process, one agreed pipeline, and one dashboard. Validate with the business before adding communications or connecting real records. Separate prototype, pilot, and production milestones in any proposal.
