# Discovery conversation

Use one generic demonstration for every prospect. Ask how the experience should adapt to their business instead of assuming their process.

- Where do customer details and appointment requests arrive?
- Who assigns staff and approves schedule changes?
- Which working-hour rules, breaks, holidays, locations, or resources affect availability?
- Which staff should receive which notifications, and when?
- What customer history is useful, and who should be able to see it?
- Which calendar, CRM, or accounting system must remain the source of truth?
- What would a successful pilot improve, and how would it be measured?

Start with customer intake, staff assignment, a team calendar, and one approved notification workflow. Agree access controls and data handling before connecting real records. The demo's emails and reminders are simulations; they do not represent live integrations.
